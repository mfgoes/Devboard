# Budget decision

The budget question affects the first version of [[Project Atlas]].

## Options

1. Keep scope small and ship the searchable document portal first.
2. Add review workflows before launch.
3. Delay until the content import is fully automated.

## Leaning

Option 1. It gives Maya the "find the thing" moment without turning the project into a six-month platform rebuild.
