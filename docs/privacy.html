Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

DevBoard is a local-first note-taking and visual thinking application.

Your files and workspaces are stored locally on your device by default. Optional cloud sync features are available if you choose to sign in.

Information We Collect

If you choose to sign in using Google, GitHub, or email authentication, DevBoard may collect your email address and basic profile information required for authentication and sync functionality.

Cloud Sync

If cloud sync is enabled, synced workspace data may be stored using Supabase infrastructure hosted in EU regions.

Local-First Philosophy

DevBoard is designed to work without an account. Your local files remain under your control.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Authentication
  • GitHub Authentication
  • Supabase
