# sdfsdf

- Dot grid background gives spatial context without noise — same as FigJam/Miro
- Card actions (expand, edit) are hidden until hover, keeping cards clean at a glance
- Wiki-links [[Tech Stack]]render as inline chips inside card previews — makes the graph nature of DevBoard visible right on the canvas
- Sticky notes get a distinct yellow treatment, differentiating them from regular note cards
- Resize handle appears on hover, bottom-right — consistent with FigJam convention
- Toolbar is pill-shaped and icon-only with a logical grouping: select/pan · content types · add
- Minimap in the bottom-left for spatial orientation when the canvas grows
- Zoom control is separate from the toolbar, bottom-right — same position users expect from Figma/FigJam
